Reset today?

Today’s completed sessions and focus minutes will be removed. Previous days are kept. This cannot be undone.

Reset settings to defaults?

Lengths, long-break interval, auto-start and sound return to their defaults and are applied right away. Unsaved changes in Settings are discarded. Your sessions and history are not affected.

Clear all history?

Every recorded session on every day will be deleted, including breaks, and your streak and stats will reset to zero. Timer and settings are kept. This cannot be undone.
